Account & Data Deletion Policy

App Name: PunahChakra | Developer Support: info.punahchakra@gmail.com

At PunahChakra, we respect your data privacy rights. Users can request the complete deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

You can delete your account directly inside the PunahChakra mobile application:

  • Open the PunahChakra App on your mobile device.
  • Tap the Profile Tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  • Tap on Personal Information / Settings.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap the red Delete Account button.
  • Confirm your selection to permanently erase your profile and sign out.
Option 2: Online / Email Request

If you cannot access the app, submit an account deletion request via email:

  • Send an email from your registered email address to info.punahchakra@gmail.com.
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - PunahChakra.
  • Include your full name and registered email address. Our support team will process your request within 48 hours.

Types of Data Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently purged from our primary database servers:

  • Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Home/Pickup Addresses.
  • Authentication Credentials & Saved Tokens.
  • Profile Settings, Active Pickup Schedules, and Preferences.

Data Retention Period & Exceptions

Anonymized transaction totals (e.g., total kilograms of material recycled and carbon offset aggregates) are retained for cumulative environmental auditing, stripped of all personal identifiers.
