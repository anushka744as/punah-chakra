Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

PunahChakra ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and platform services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that enables us to provide doorstep waste collection, route optimization, and circular economy tracking:

  • Personal Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and delivery/pickup address provided during registration or profile updates.
  • Recycling & Donation Data: Scheduled pickup categories (clothes, shoes, blankets/bedsheets, bags), estimated and verified item weights, selected time slots, and historical recycling activity logs.
  • Location Data: Doorstep pickup addresses and precise geolocation data for driver route navigation during active collection tasks.
  • Authentication Data: User account identifiers managed securely through Supabase Authentication services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for operational, environmental, and support purposes:

  • To process, schedule, and complete doorstep recyclable waste pickups.
  • To lock and coordinate driver route assignments, preventing duplicate dispatches.
  • To compute your environmental metrics, including waste tonnage diverted.
  • To verify material purity and audit quality logs for industrial recycling facilities.
  • To communicate real-time pickup status notifications and respond to customer support inquiries via info.punahchakra@gmail.com.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Information is only shared under the following conditions:

  • Logistics Drivers & Collectors: Necessary pickup details (address, material category, time slot) are shared with confirmed collectors to facilitate collection.
  • Cloud Services: We use Supabase for secure encrypted cloud data storage, authentication, and database management.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Disclosure required by law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All database communications are transmitted via TLS/SSL encryption.

5. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal information stored on PunahChakra. To submit a data deletion request or export your account information, please contact us at info.punahchakra@gmail.com.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the PunahChakra application after any modifications constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
